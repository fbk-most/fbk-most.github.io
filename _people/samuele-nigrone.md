Samuele Nigrone is a PhD student at the [MoST research unit](/) and at the department of Psychology and Cognitive Science, University of Trento.

His current doctoral research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction, in particular on the study of cognitive and motivational mechanisms to increase active civic engagement within Urban Digital Twins. 

Samuele earned a Master's Degree in **Human-Computer Interaction** from the University of Trento, centering on human-AI interaction, user research, and gamification strategies.
